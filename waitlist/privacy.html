Privacy notice

Last updated 5 October 2026

This notice explains how Oh my Gogh! handles your information when you join our waitlist, browse the site, or use an artist account on the marketplace. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The short version: waitlist emails are used only to tell you about our launch. Artist profiles and approved listings are public on the site once approved. We use no analytics or advertising cookies, only the strictly necessary session cookie for logged-in artists.

Who is responsible

The data fiduciary (DPDP Act) and data controller (GDPR) is Sparx Consultancy (proprietorship of Parth Sharma), trading as Oh my Gogh!. You can reach us at hello@ohmygogh.com. This address is also our contact point for privacy questions and grievances.

Visitors who only browse

If you only browse the website, we do not collect personal data from you beyond standard server logs held by Cloudflare. We do not add our own analytics or advertising tools for those visits.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we collect:

  • Your email address, which you type into the waitlist form.
  • A record of when you signed up and which form on the site you used. This is our proof that you asked to join.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, address or payment details on the waitlist.

We use your email only to send you updates about the launch of Oh my Gogh!, such as when the store opens. We will not use your email for anything unrelated, and we will not sell it or rent it to anyone.

The legal basis is your consent. By submitting the form you give a clear, specific, voluntary agreement to receive launch updates (GDPR Article 6(1)(a); DPDP Act section 6). You can withdraw it at any time.

We keep your email until you withdraw your consent, or until 24 months after you signed up, whichever comes first. After that your email is deleted. If we want to keep in touch beyond that time we will ask you again.

To protect the form from abuse, our server briefly keeps a counter linked to a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address. It is deleted automatically within minutes and is never used to identify you.

Artist accounts

If you create an artist account, we collect the details you give us and the records we need to run it:

  • your email address;
  • your password, stored only as a salted hash (we do not store the password itself);
  • your name, handle, bio, location, category and tags, and links;
  • your avatar;
  • your listings, with titles, descriptions, prices and images;
  • session data used to keep you logged in;
  • admin review decisions and notes.

Your profile and approved listing content are public on the website once approved. A profile stays private until the artist account is approved, and a listing stays private until that listing is approved. Your email address, password hash, session data and private review notes are not published.

We use artist account data for running accounts, moderation, displaying listings, security, and legal compliance. We do not sell it or rent it.

The legal basis is the account you ask us to provide (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), including moderation and display of listings you submit. Security, including rate limits, relies on our legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Where the law requires a record, we rely on legal compliance (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)). Under the DPDP Act you provide this data voluntarily for those specified purposes (section 7). You can stop by deleting your account. Where a separate consent is required, we will ask for it.

Cookies

We use no analytics or advertising cookies, only the strictly necessary session cookie for logged-in artists. We also use no advertising pixels or tracking scripts. That cookie is HttpOnly, it is used only to keep you logged in, and it lasts 14 days. We do not set it when you join the waitlist or when you browse without an artist login.

Rate limits

To protect accounts and forms from abuse, we keep rate-limit counters using a hashed IP address. They are deleted within hours and are never used to identify you. The waitlist form counter described above is deleted within minutes.

Payments

No payments are processed yet. We do not collect card numbers or other payment details. When payments are added, this notice will be updated before they go live.

Who processes it

Our website, waitlist storage, database and images run on Cloudflare, which acts on our behalf as a processor: hosting, the D1 database, and R2 image storage. Your data may therefore be processed outside India and outside your country, on servers operated by Cloudflare. When we begin sending launch emails we will use an email service provider acting under our instructions. We do not share your personal data with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails are kept until you withdraw your consent, or until 24 months after you signed up, whichever comes first. After that your email is deleted.

Artist account data is kept until you delete the account with the self-service delete in the dashboard, or after prolonged inactivity. Deleting the account removes the profile, listings, images and sessions tied to it.

Rate-limit data is deleted within hours. The admin log is kept while the account exists, plus a reasonable period after that. Session data lasts until you log out, you delete the account, or the session cookie expires after 14 days, whichever comes first.

Your rights and how to withdraw

To withdraw consent, unsubscribe or have your email deleted, write to hello@ohmygogh.com from the address you signed up with. We will act on it within 30 days, and usually much sooner. If you have an artist account, you can erase it yourself with the self-service delete in the dashboard, or ask us by the same email.

You also have the right to:

  • ask what data we hold about you and get a copy (access);
  • have inaccurate data corrected and have your data erased (erasure);
  • receive the data you provided in a portable format (portability);
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you cannot (DPDP Act).

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority (for people in the EU/UK) or to the Data Protection Board of India. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

This waitlist, and artist accounts, are meant for adults. Please do not sign up if you are under 18. If you believe a child has given us their email or opened an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where needed, ask for your consent again.
