Our story

For people who notice the color in everything.

Oh my Gogh! is a home for people who stop to look at the light on a wall, keep paint under their nails and treat ordinary things as a canvas.

We are building a small world of wearable art, tools for making, and objects for the studio. It all starts from one feeling: color is how you say hello to the day.

Warm, bold and a little bit messy, in the best way.
